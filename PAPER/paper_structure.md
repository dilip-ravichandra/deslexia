# Research Paper Structure

1. Abstract
2. Introduction
3. Problem Statement
4. Related Work / Literature Survey
5. Research Gap
6. Research Questions and Hypotheses
7. Dataset and Annotation
8. Methodology
9. Baselines
10. DistilBERT
11. Linguistic Feature Fusion
12. Targeted Intervention
13. Semantic Verification
14. Experimental Setup
15. Results
16. Error Analysis
17. Human Evaluation
18. Discussion
19. Limitations
20. Ethics
21. Conclusion
22. References

Results must be generated from experiment artifacts.
