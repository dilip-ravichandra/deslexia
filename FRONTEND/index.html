About your research project

The reader combines your current request, saved learner evidence and local explanation models when installed. Uploads support PDF extraction and OCR. Picture explanations use a separate vision model and need checking against the source. Public lexical datasets and human support-routing annotations are evaluated separately. Student benefit has not been evaluated.

Automatic meaning checks can miss errors. Practice passages are original examples, not a research dataset. Signed-in saved passages and notes are in the helper database; guest saves stay in this browser.
