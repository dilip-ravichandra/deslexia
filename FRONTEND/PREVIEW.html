About your research project

The reading interface is functional. The current practice helper uses rules, a small demonstration word list, and guiding questions. A validated classifier and conceptual explanation model are not loaded.

Automatic meaning checks can miss errors. Practice passages are original examples, not a research dataset. Saved passages and notes live in this browser; clearing browser storage removes them.
