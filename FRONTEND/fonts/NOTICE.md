Noto Sans Devanagari, Noto Sans Kannada and Noto Sans Tamil are bundled from the official google/fonts repository. Their original SIL Open Font License notices accompany each font. These fonts provide local script coverage; they are not claimed to be clinically superior fonts for dyslexia. Native-script reader text keeps normal letter spacing to preserve shaping. Font size, line spacing, colours and width remain adjustable.
