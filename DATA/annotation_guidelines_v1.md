# Annotation Guidelines v1

Given a learner-selected span and local context, assign the most appropriate computational support category.

## VOCABULARY
The main difficulty is lexical: unfamiliar word, technical term, morphology or expression.

## SYNTACTIC
The main difficulty is sentence structure: clause nesting, passive construction, long dependency, grammatical relation or structural complexity.

## CONCEPTUAL
The main difficulty is understanding the idea, relation, process or implication rather than one word or sentence structure.

## OTHER_UNCERTAIN
Use when context is insufficient or none of the primary categories is defensible.

## Rules
- Read the whole sentence.
- Read surrounding context.
- Label the selected span in context.
- Do not diagnose dyslexia.
- Do not label a medical cause.
- Record uncertainty.
- AI suggestions cannot silently become gold labels.
