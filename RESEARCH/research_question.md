# Research Question

Can localized, learner-guided intervention provide useful reading support while changing fewer text units and preserving more original content than uniform simplification?

## Secondary questions
1. Can a compact classifier route selected spans into Vocabulary, Syntactic or Conceptual support?
2. Does feature fusion improve over transformer-only classification?
3. Does targeted intervention reduce unnecessary rewriting?
4. How do learner-controlled visual accessibility settings affect perceived reading comfort?

These are research questions, not pre-claimed findings.
